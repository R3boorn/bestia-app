The training system of
Głodne Wilki

BESTIA builds training plans for obstacle course racing (OCR) athletes from the data recorded by their Garmin watches and delivers each workout straight to the watch. Running, strength, grip and recovery in one plan, built around real races.

The team

Głodne Wilki

An OCR and Ninja team from Lublin, Poland, organised as the Głodne Wilki association (Stowarzyszenie Głodne Wilki), founded in November 2024, with athletes from across the country. Team members race at the biggest obstacle races in Poland, including Runmageddon, Barbarian Race, Spartan Race, Wolf Race and Vikings Run, compete in Ninja Series and Ninja Warrior Polska, and also run road and mountain races and triathlons.

The team trains together in Lublin twice a week with coaches; the rest of the training is done individually. BESTIA is built to guide exactly those individual sessions.

For team athletes

What BESTIA does

Long OCR events, up to 45 km with more than 140 obstacles, need a plan that combines trail running, strength, grip and rest. BESTIA builds it for each athlete individually.

Plan on the watch

Step-by-step workouts

Every workout is sent to the Garmin Connect calendar and the watch: warm-up, intervals with pace or heart-rate targets, recoveries, strength and grip steps with alerts.

Goals and records

Visible progress

Season goals on the first screen, distance records, strength and grip tests, and a review of every training block.

Recovery

A plan that fits the day

HRV, resting heart rate and sleep from the watch help decide whether to train as planned or go lighter.

  1. Connect

    The athlete connects a Garmin Connect account and gives consent.

  2. Data

    BESTIA reads activities and daily metrics exactly as Garmin reports them.

  3. Plan

    An evidence-based plan is delivered to the watch via Garmin Connect.

  4. Review

    After each workout BESTIA checks execution and adjusts the next sessions.

Status: closed beta. BESTIA runs as a working prototype; the first test group is the Głodne Wilki team.

Data and artificial intelligence

Your data serves only you

  • BESTIA uses Anthropic's Claude language model to prepare plans and summaries, only from the athlete's own data and only after the athlete's separate, explicit consent.
  • Consent can be withdrawn at any time. Without it, no data is sent to AI.
  • We do not train or fine-tune any AI model on Garmin data or on athletes' data.
  • No advertising, no sale of data. Garmin-sourced data is attributed to Garmin.
