Artificial intelligence in BESTIA: key facts

BESTIA uses Anthropic's Claude language model (via API) to prepare each user's training plan and summaries from that user's own data, including Garmin data.

  1. AI processing starts only after the user gives separate, explicit consent, distinct from any other consent.
  2. Consent can be withdrawn at any time with one click in BESTIA settings or by e-mail to zarzad@mimariposa.pl. Without it, BESTIA sends no data to AI and no plan is generated automatically.
  3. A user's data is used only to answer that same user.
  4. We do not use Garmin data, anything derived from it, or any other user data to train, fine-tune, evaluate or improve any AI model, ours or third parties'. Anthropic does not train its models on data sent through its commercial API.
  5. AI output is a proposal that the user approves or changes.
  6. We update this statement whenever the law or Garmin's policies change.

2. What BESTIA is

BESTIA is a training planning system for obstacle course racing (OCR) athletes, currently offered as a closed, non-commercial beta to athletes of the Głodne Wilki team. Using data from the athlete's Garmin watch, it builds a training plan, sends workouts to Garmin Connect and prepares progress summaries.

3. Data we process

  1. Data from the Garmin Connect account that the user chooses to share by connecting it: activities (date, duration, distance, elevation, heart rate, cadence and other running metrics) and daily metrics (HRV, resting heart rate, sleep, body weight).
  2. Data entered by the user: goals, race calendar, race results, tests, survey answers.
  3. Contact data: first name and e-mail address.

Our Garmin integration also includes the Women's Health API (menstrual cycle and pregnancy tracking data, only for users who record it in Garmin Connect and allow its sharing) and the Courses API (sending routes to Garmin Connect). BESTIA does not currently use either of them: no women's health data is collected and no routes are sent. If we start using them, we will update this policy beforehand, and women's health data will be processed only with the user's separate, explicit consent, only to adapt that user's training, and never for AI processing without the AI consent described above. The user controls which data types Garmin shares with BESTIA in the Garmin Connect account settings.

Heart rate, HRV, sleep, body weight and women's health data may be data concerning health within the meaning of Art. 9 GDPR.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  1. Preparing and sending the training plan, progress analysis: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and explicit consent to process health data (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR).
  2. Preparing plans and summaries using artificial intelligence: separate explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR), described in the box at the top of this policy.
  3. Security and technical support: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Consent can be withdrawn at any time by disconnecting Garmin in BESTIA or in Garmin Connect, or by e-mail. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing before it.

5. Recipients

Data may be processed by service providers acting on our behalf: Garmin (data source and recipient of training plans), Google (spreadsheets and scripts), GitHub (file storage), Anthropic (language model). We do not sell data or share it for marketing.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Some providers are based in the USA. Transfers are based on the European Commission adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

7. Retention

We keep data while the user uses BESTIA and delete it within 30 days after the account is disconnected or consent is withdrawn, unless the law requires longer storage.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete and export (port) your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent. To exercise these rights, disconnect Garmin in BESTIA or in Garmin Connect, or write to zarzad@mimariposa.pl; we respond within 30 days. You may lodge a complaint with the Polish supervisory authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa).

9. Security

Access keys are stored only in protected script settings, never in files or spreadsheets. Access to data is limited to people operating BESTIA.
